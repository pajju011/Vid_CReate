# YouTube Agent Skills — 11 Creator Skills for Content Production

This skill equips the agent with 11 specialized content creation workflows and analysis algorithms for YouTube creators, YouTube Shorts, and viral video engineering.

## 11 Creator Workflows Available

### 1. `/yt-script` — High-Retention Scriptwriting
- Transforms an idea into a complete production script.
- Generates 5 distinct opening hooks based on 21 viral hook formulas (curiosity gap, paradox, stake-raising, contrarian, immediate proof).
- Embeds spoken cues, pacing markers, and visual b-roll triggers every 4–7 seconds to prevent drop-off.

### 2. `/yt-package` — Title & Thumbnail Synergy Linter
- Evaluates title and thumbnail as an integrated visual-verbal package.
- Enforces zero text redundancy between title and thumbnail text.
- Checks character limits (<50 characters for mobile display without truncation).
- Evaluates emotional curiosity vs clarity balance.

### 3. `/yt-edit` — Transcript & SRT Edit Decision List (EDL)
- Parses transcript or SRT files to locate dead air (>1.2s pauses).
- Identifies verbal fillers ("um", "uh", "like", "you know", "actually").
- Detects stammered retakes and outputs an Edit Decision List (cut timestamps).

### 4. `/yt-retention` — Audience Retention Diagnostics
- Ingests retention CSV data from YouTube Studio.
- Diagnoses:
  - 30-second hook drop-off (intro leak).
  - Mid-video drop cliffs (boredom or unfulfilled promises).
  - Scrub spikes (high-value moments replayed).
- Generates concrete script and editing fixes.

### 5. `/yt-shorts` — Long-Form to Viral Shorts Extractor
- Scans long-form video transcripts for standalone high-engagement moments (30–60s).
- Adds high-curiosity spoken opening hooks to hook viewers within 1.5 seconds.
- Formats scenes for 9:16 vertical delivery.

### 6. `/yt-viral` — Channel-Relative Outlier Detection
- Analyzes niche competitors using Channel-Relative View Multiples ($V_{video} / V_{median}$).
- Isolates true viral outliers from baseline channel performance.

### 7. `/yt-seo` — Search & Algorithmic Optimization
- Generates search-intent descriptions, semantic tags, and chapter markers.
- Optimizes for high CTR browse and suggested video features.

### 8. `/yt-chapters` — YouTube Player Chapter Generator
- Generates timestamped chapters formatted strictly for YouTube's player (`00:00 Intro`, etc.).
- Verifies monotonicity, minimum 3 chapters, and at least 10s chapter length.

### 9. `/yt-comment` — Community Engagement Engine
- Triages comments into 4 buckets: Superfans, Constructive Feedback, Confusion/FAQ, Trolls.
- Drafts authentic, high-engagement replies in the creator's distinct voice.

### 10. `/yt-plan` — Sustainable Weekly Production Calendar
- Structures an efficient weekly publishing rhythm: 1 Anchor Long-form, 1 Low-lift Video, 3 Repurposed Shorts.
- Prevents creator burnout while maximizing YouTube algorithm momentum.

### 11. `/yt-audit` — Comprehensive Channel Bottleneck Audit
- Performs an end-to-end audit (Packaging, Hook Rate, Retention, Upload Cadence).
- Concludes with the single highest-leverage bottleneck to fix first.
