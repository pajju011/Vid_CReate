# AI Video Generation — Multi-Model Generative Video Engine

End-to-end procedural AI video generation suite orchestrating leading foundation models for cinematic Text-to-Video (T2V), Image-to-Video (I2V), and character animation.

## Supported Model Matrix

| Model Tier | Flagship Models | Best Use Case | Native Resolutions / Formats |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1: Cloud Foundation** | Google Veo 3.1, Veo 3 | High-fidelity cinematic realism, complex lighting, temporal stability | 1080p, 4K, 16:9 & 9:16 |
| **Tier 2: Character & Avatar** | Seedance 2.0, OmniHuman | Expressive human faces, conversational gestures, audio-driven lipsync | 1080x1920 (9:16 vertical) |
| **Tier 3: Motion & Physics** | Wan 2.5, HunyuanVideo | Complex fluid mechanics, fire, cloth dynamics, fast camera movement | 720p, 1080p |
| **Tier 4: Creative & Stylized** | Grok Imagine Video, HappyHorse | Bold surreal concepts, high-contrast dynamic action | 16:9 & 9:16 |

## Operational Capabilities
1. **Text-to-Video (T2V)**: Translates natural language prompts into motion-controlled video clips with specified camera angles (push-in, pan, drone tracking, Dutch angle).
2. **Image-to-Video (I2V)**: Animates still concept art or photorealistic keyframes while preserving character identity and spatial geometry.
3. **Reference-to-Video (R2V)**: Maintains temporal character, wardrobe, and environment consistency across sequential multi-shot scenes.
4. **AI Upscaling & Motion Smoothing**: Enhances 720p generations to 4K resolution with temporal de-noising and 60fps frame interpolation.
5. **AI Foley & Audio Alignment**: Automatically generates synchronized sound effects and ambient atmospheric audio matching the visual action.
