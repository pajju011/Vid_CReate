# AI Video Generation Instructions & Prompt Engineering SOP

## 1. Cinematic Prompt Engineering Structure
To achieve maximum realism across foundation models (Veo, Wan, Seedance):
`[Subject & Action] + [Camera Motion & Lens] + [Lighting & Atmosphere] + [Environment & Setting] + [Style & Quality Markers]`

*Example (Cinematic Food Reel):*
> "Macro close-up shot of a thick gourmet beef patty sizzling on a smoking cast-iron grill, melted sharp cheddar dripping down the sides. Slow push-in camera movement, 35mm anamorphic lens, warm golden hour restaurant lighting, shallow depth of field, 8k photorealistic detail."

## 2. Aspect Ratio & Resolution Standards
- **Vertical Reels & Shorts**: `1080x1920` (9:16 aspect ratio).
- **Cinematic Landscape**: `1920x1080` or `3840x2160` (16:9 aspect ratio).
- **Square Showcase**: `1080x1080` (1:1 aspect ratio).

## 3. Motion & Camera Physics Parameters
- **Pan / Tracking**: Smooth continuous horizontal translation at constant velocity.
- **Push-In / Zoom**: +5% to +10% focal length scaling with subtle foreground parallax.
- **Drone / Crane**: High-angle downward descent with rotational reveal.
