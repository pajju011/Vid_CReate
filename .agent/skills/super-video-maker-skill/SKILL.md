# Super Video Maker Skill — End-to-End AI Video Production Pipeline

This skill integrates code-driven video synthesis, Remotion/React motion layouts, FFmpeg visual compositing, and automated audio-visual alignment.

## Core Production Playbooks & Capabilities

### 1. Code-Driven React Video & Motion Graphics (Remotion / HyperFrames)
- Component-driven keyframe animation and physics-based spring transitions.
- Kinetic typography layouts with word-by-word active highlighting.
- Split-screen video arrangements (e.g. Avatar talking head + dynamic b-roll + captions).
- Automated audio synchronization using Whisper word timestamps.

### 2. High-Performance FFmpeg Compositing Engine
- 9:16 vertical reels and shorts format adaptation with ambient blurred canvas padding.
- Picture-in-Picture (PiP) layering with drop shadows, rounded borders, and alpha channels.
- Two-pass automated audio ducking: background music drops by -14dB during voiceover narration.
- EBU R128 loudness normalization (-14 LUFS for YouTube, -16 LUFS for Instagram/TikTok).

### 3. High-Conversion Video Scriptwriting & Hook Architecture
- Hook engineering: 3-second pattern interrupt formulas (curiosity gaps, stakes, paradoxes).
- Spoken VO humanization: conversational cadences, natural breath marks, and emotional rhythm.
- Visual pacing rule: mandatory visual stimulation or camera shift every 3–5 seconds.

### 4. Modular Video Workflows
- **`avatar-insta-reel`**: Talking head avatar synced with full-screen cinematic b-roll.
- **`review-video`**: Software / SaaS UI review walkthrough with zoomed callouts and badges.
- **`tabletop-explainer`**: Overhead desk perspective presenting products and physical items.
- **`motion-collage`**: Mixed-media paper cut and stop-motion dynamic collage aesthetics.
